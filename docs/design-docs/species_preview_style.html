Species Image Preview — where the culling signals live

The culling card is the framed card plus four signals the Trip/Master/Album cards don't carry: species-ID status (OK / uncertain / none — clickable to fix), GPS present/missing, crop, description, and a location line. Same photo frame, same clickable stars/colours. The open question is placement — pick a variant and a colour scheme to compare.

Attention-only keeps the card clean and only flags what needs a decision — a red frame + corner when GPS is missing or the species is unset/uncertain; crop/description sit as faint marks on the filename line. Best for fast culling: a tidy card means "nothing to fix here."
On the image pins GPS to the top-right corner always (📍/❓) and shows the ID status as a pill by the name; crop/📝 ride the filename line. Everything visible without a strip, at the cost of busier photos.
Badge strip below is today's row — status · ⌗ · 📝 · 📍 under the card, corners stay minimal. Most familiar, most explicit, but adds a row of height per card.

Still to come: the large image view (single-photo viewer + filmstrip) and the map view bubbles/strips — both reuse these signals, so whichever placement you pick here sets the vocabulary for those too.
