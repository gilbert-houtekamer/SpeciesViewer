# 2. Browser

One menu, one idea: **viewing a collection**. The same two views over each of the
three scopes that have a collection worth browsing. It shows your own
pictures, so in a new master there is nothing to browse yet.

Think of it as your own website, but local and complete — every species and
every picture, not only the ones that have a JPG and made it to the website.
Browsing is not a
pipeline step, so it has a menu of its own, apart from the trip and album
actions.

| View | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| **Browser** | The taxonomy tree — Kingdom → Phylum → … → Species. Drill down to a species to see its pictures. |
| **All Species** | A flat, searchable index of every species in the scope. Click one to see its pictures. |
| **Map** | Where the pictures were taken, and the route you travelled. A trip or album map shows every picture; the Species Map shows one to four species of the master. |

The browsers are **read-only**: nothing you do in them changes data. The maps
change one thing only: they let you **place pictures that have no GPS**. To edit
anything else, use
the corresponding Species Viewer ([Trips](04-trips.md), [Albums](07-albums.md))
or the master's [Species Viewer](06-master.md).

---

## Trip Browser… / All Species in the Trip…

*Needs: an active trip (or a configured standalone repository).*

The active trip's species, by taxonomy or as a flat list. This shows every
species the trip has identified. The trip website shows the ones with a
developed JPG — usually fewer.

## Album Browser… / All Species in the Album…

*Needs: an active album.*

The same two views over the active album. Remember that an album is a 
date-range window on the master, so what you see here is the master's own
content, filtered to those dates. Nothing is copied.

## Master Browser… / All Species in the Master…

*Needs: a configured master.*

The whole collection. This is the browser you will use most: it is the answer to
"have I photographed this before, and what did I get?"

## Trip Map… / Album Map… / Species Map…

*Needs: an active trip / an active album / a master.*

The pictures on a map. **Trip Map…** and **Album Map…** show every picture of the
trip or album, with the travel path. **Species Map…** shows one to four species
of the master, each in its own colour. All three are described in
[10. The viewers ▸ The map](10-viewers.md#the-map).

---

## Choosing between the two views

- Use the **Browser** when the taxonomy itself is the question — *what warblers
  do I have?* — or when you want to see how much of a family you have covered.
- Use **All Species** when you know roughly what you are after and want to type
  it. It has a search box.

Both end at the same place: a species and its pictures.

A few things hold in both views:

- **Order follows the species list**, not the alphabet: families and species
  appear in the order of the bird and mammal lists SpeciesViewer uses, so related
  species sit together.
- **A subspecies sits right after its species** and counts as that species —
  photographing three subspecies of one bird is still one species.
- **Countries** are shown in your own language, whatever the place name in the
  picture says. Click a country chip on a species to open its map zoomed to that
  country.
