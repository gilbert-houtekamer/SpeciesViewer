SpeciesViewer - sample pictures
===============================

One day of a nature trip: 9 September 2026, the Pantanal, Brazil.
Ten pictures as they come off the card - Olympus RAW files (.ORF), an iPhone
landscape (.HEIC) and a short video (.MOV) - with:

  - the .xmp sidecars, holding the species names and colour labels;
  - five JPGs made from the RAW files (DxO PhotoLab) and one from the HEIC;
  - GPS positions, so the pictures appear on the map.

The species: western osprey, black howler (a monkey), rufescent tiger heron,
capped heron, turkey vulture, a dragonfly (Diastatops pullata), jaguar and a
proboscis bat - plus one landscape.


Trying them in SpeciesViewer
----------------------------
1. Unpack this ZIP to a folder, for example C:\SpeciesViewer-sample.
2. In SpeciesViewer: Days > Copy day from card..., and choose that folder
   (or: Master > Add Pictures to Master..., to put them straight into your
   master collection).
3. All pictures land on 2026-09-09. Open the day to see them, their species
   and colours; Map shows where they were taken.
